# QuoteCard
The NGN social pull-quote treatment — display-serif quote over the arrow watermark. `tone="dark"` (black IG story) or `"cream"` (warm square post); `format="story"` (9:16) or `"square"` (1:1). Composes ArrowPattern.

```jsx
<QuoteCard tone="dark" format="story" assetBase="../../assets"
  quote="Because we've been doing this coordination with the city and university for so long, we've got it down."
  name="Mallory Pernaa" role="director of off-campus engagement and support at Northeastern" />
```
Set `assetBase` to reach /assets from your page. Quote goes in the display serif; the name is bold, the role regular.
